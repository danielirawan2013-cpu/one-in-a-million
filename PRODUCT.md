# One in a Million
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Delegated by user: static HTML/CSS/JavaScript with a Canvas 2D game. GitHub-compatible source and GitHub Pages deployment.

## Users
The user wants to create and play their first pixel adventure game.

## Product Purpose
A playable first chapter about a human shopkeeper who looks weak but has one-in-a-million dormant strength. Rescuing his adopted monster family unlocks his potential.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Top-down exploration chosen by implementer at user's request. Keyboard and touch movement, combat, loot chests with rare and trash drops, earned power progression, family dialogue, and chapter completion. The protagonist is human, not a goblin. Funny with heart. User wants source uploaded on GitHub. Pixel art is drawn directly in code at user's request.

## Brand Commitments
Working title One in a Million. Protagonist Pip: small human, messy hair, oversized shopkeeper apron and battered sword. Monster family raised him.

## Product Principles
- A complete playable chapter before expanding the world.
- Strength is earned, and essential progression never depends on random loot.
- The hero remains physically small as his power grows.
- Trash loot has value at the shop.
